DARLING.EXE V4 AUDIO SETUP

Place your own legally obtained/truncated audio files in this folder with these exact names:

1) sunflower.mp3
   General background music from the first page through the game.
   Recommended: a subtle/quiet segment.

2) birthday-bridge.mp3
   The birthday chapter music, starting when the Birthday Reveal appears.
   Recommended: your chosen bridge/soft segment of the song.

3) photograph-bridge.mp3
   The monthsary chapter music, starting when US, DARLING appears.
   Recommended: your chosen bridge/soft segment of Keenan Te's Photograph.

The app switches these tracks automatically when the relevant screen appears.

IMPORTANT:
Mobile browsers can block audio autoplay before the user interacts with the page. DARLING.EXE
attempts to start the first track automatically, then retries on the first tap/click/keypress.
After that interaction, the chapter switches are automatic.

The game also has built-in synthesized sound effects, so no SFX files are required.
